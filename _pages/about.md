Hi, thanks for stopping by my page. I am Nga-Man Wong from Hong Kong. I am currently an undergraduate student at The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK), pursuing a Bachelor of Education (Honours) in Business, Accounting and Financial Studies.

My research interests focus on designing AI-enabled and data-informed learning environments and education policies to improve student engagement, formative assessment, self-directed learning, and educational equity. Methodologically, I am interested in mixed methods, survey research, qualitative interviewing, focus groups, thematic analysis, learning analytics, computational text analysis, and applied quantitative analysis.

I am preparing for PhD applications for Fall 2027. You can find my [CV](images/resume.pdf) here.
